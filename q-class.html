Audi Q Class

Q Class

The Q Class brings Audi’s luxury and technology to a versatile SUV platform designed for daily life, family comfort, and all-weather confidence.

Audi Q family

Compact and Nimble

Audi Q2 is the compact member of the Q family, offering agile handling, practical dimensions, and a distinctive design suited to urban environments. Its compact proportions make it easy to maneuver while maintaining the premium character associated with Audi.

Versatile and Modern

Audi Q3 sits above the Q2 and provides a larger, more versatile SUV experience. It combines modern design, spacious interior accommodation, advanced digital technology, and balanced performance for both city driving and longer journeys.

Electric Future

Audi Q4 e-tron brings the Q family into the fully electric segment. It combines SUV practicality with electric performance, a spacious cabin, progressive styling, and modern digital technology. The Q4 e-tron is available in different configurations designed around efficiency, performance, and everyday usability.

The Core SUV

Audi Q5 is a midsize premium SUV focused on comfort, versatility, and long-distance capability. Its refined interior, practical proportions, advanced technology, and available quattro all-wheel drive make it one of Audi’s central SUV models.

Next-Generation Electric

Audi Q6 e-tron represents Audi’s next generation of electric SUVs. Built around a dedicated electric platform, it combines high-performance electric powertrains with advanced charging technology, digital interfaces, sophisticated lighting, and a spacious premium interior.

Large Luxury Family SUV

Audi Q7 is a large luxury SUV designed for maximum versatility and passenger space. Its spacious cabin, advanced driver assistance technology, refined ride, and available three-row configuration make it suitable for families, long journeys, and premium everyday transportation.

Flagship Presence

Audi Q8 is the flagship of the Q family, combining the presence of a large SUV with a more dynamic coupé-inspired silhouette. Its design emphasizes broad proportions, premium materials, advanced technology, and powerful performance, creating a more distinctive alternative to the traditional Q7.
