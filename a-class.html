Audi A Class

A Class

The A Class represents Audi’s compact luxury vision: refined design, everyday comfort, and intelligent performance in a modern executive format.

Audi A family

The Entry Point

The Audi A1 is the smallest model in the A family and is positioned as the entry-level Audi. The second-generation A1 Sportback is a five-door hatchback built for city use and youthful driving. It is compact, easy to handle, and more focused on everyday practicality than on showing off size or presence.

The Electric Revival

The A2 is a special case in Audi history. The original A2 was a compact aluminium-bodied hatchback produced from 1999 to 2005, while the modern A2 e-tron concept represents the electric revival of the name. In today’s lineup, it sits alongside models such as the A6 e-tron, Q4 e-tron, Q6 e-tron, and e-tron GT, showing how Audi reinterprets compact mobility in the EV era.

Compact Premium

The A3 is one of the most important models in the A lineup because it offers a compact premium experience without stepping up too far in size. It includes the A3 Sportback, A3 Saloon, and A3 TFSI e variants, while performance variants such as the S3 and RS3 demonstrate how Audi builds sportiness into a smaller body. It is the model that sits between city practicality and serious premium appeal.

The Executive Classic

The A4 is a classic midsize executive Audi and one of the brand’s most iconic models. Historically, it has existed as a sedan, Avant, and allroad, with S4 and RS4 variants adding performance. Audi later restructured its lineup, and the A5 took over a large part of the role once occupied by the combustion A4. This shift reflects how Audi is moving toward a more modern range structure while still preserving the legacy of the A4.

The Modern Successor

The A5 now represents the modern successor to the old A4 role in the combustion era. It is available in the Saloon, Avant, e-hybrid, S5, and RS5 forms, bringing together practicality, a more expressive design, and premium performance. In short, the A5 combines the everyday usability that defined the A4 with a more stylish and modern personality.

Executive Luxury

The A6 is the executive luxury model in the family. It is larger and more refined than the A5, with the A6 Saloon, A6 Avant, A6 allroad, and e-hybrid versions forming the core of the range. Audi also builds the A6 e-tron family, which includes Sportback and Avant electric variants and shows how the executive-class Audi experience is now expanding into electrification without losing the premium feel that defines the A6.

The Sportback Statement

The A7 is slightly different from the A6 because it is built as a luxury Sportback rather than a traditional sedan. Its fastback roofline gives it a more coupe-like silhouette while still keeping the executive comfort and technology expected from Audi. The A7 Sportback, S7 Sportback, and RS7 Sportback Performance represent the more expressive and performance-focused side of the A family.

Flagship Prestige

At the top of the A range sits the A8. This is Audi’s flagship sedan and the clearest expression of its full-size executive luxury philosophy. With A8 and A8 L versions, plus the S8 performance model, it competes directly with the most prestigious executive sedans in the world. It is the most luxurious and commanding expression of the A family, defining the standard for high-end Audi comfort, technology, and prestige.
