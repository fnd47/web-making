Audi S Class

S Class

The S Class offers a sharper, sportier interpretation of Audi luxury, pairing executive comfort with precise handling and stronger output.

Audi S family

Compact Performance

Audi S3 is the performance version of the A3. It combines compact dimensions with increased power, quattro all-wheel drive, sport-tuned suspension, and a more aggressive exterior design. The S3 is designed to deliver strong performance while remaining practical enough for everyday driving.

Executive Sport

Audi S5 is the performance-focused version of the A5 family. It offers a more powerful engine, quattro all-wheel drive, sportier suspension, larger brakes, and distinctive S styling. Available in modern body configurations such as the S5 Saloon and S5 Avant, it combines executive comfort with a more dynamic character.

High-Performance Executive

Audi S6 is the performance variant of the A6. It provides stronger acceleration, enhanced chassis technology, quattro all-wheel drive, and subtle exterior changes that distinguish it from the standard A6. The S6 is designed to balance high-speed capability with long-distance comfort.

Grand Touring Luxury

Audi S7 is the performance version of the A7 Sportback. Its combination of a sleek Sportback body, powerful drivetrain, quattro all-wheel drive, and performance-oriented chassis gives it a grand touring character. The S7 sits between everyday luxury and serious performance.

Flagship Performance

Audi S8 represents the performance flagship of the A8 range. It combines the spaciousness and luxury of a full-size executive sedan with a powerful drivetrain, advanced suspension technology, quattro all-wheel drive, and understated S design elements. Unlike an RS model, the S8 maintains a relatively discreet appearance while delivering substantial performance.
