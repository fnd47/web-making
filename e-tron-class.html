Audi E-Tron Class

E-Tron Class

The E-Tron Class showcases Audi’s electric future, combining silent acceleration, advanced software, and premium comfort in one modern platform.

Audi electric range

Compact Electric SUV

Audi Q4 e-tron is a compact electric SUV designed for everyday use. It combines the practicality of the Q family with a fully electric powertrain, spacious interior, modern digital technology, and distinctive aerodynamic styling. The Q4 e-tron is also available as the sleeker Q4 Sportback e-tron.

Next-Generation Electric SUV

Audi Q6 e-tron is a next-generation electric SUV positioned between the Q4 e-tron and larger Q8 models. It introduces Audi’s newer electric architecture, advanced charging capabilities, sophisticated lighting technology, and a highly digital interior. The Q6 e-tron is designed to combine long-distance capability with strong electric performance.

Executive Electric Luxury

Audi A6 e-tron brings fully electric technology into the executive segment. Available as the A6 Sportback e-tron and A6 Avant e-tron, it focuses on aerodynamic efficiency, long-distance range, premium comfort, and advanced technology. Its sleek proportions are designed around reducing aerodynamic resistance and maximizing electric efficiency.

Performance with Comfort

Audi S6 e-tron adds a stronger performance character to the A6 e-tron family. It combines dual-motor electric performance, quattro all-wheel drive, sportier chassis tuning, and distinctive S design elements while maintaining the comfort and practicality of the A6 e-tron.

Electric Grand Tourer

Audi e-tron GT is Audi’s electric grand tourer and one of the most performance-focused vehicles in the electric range. Its low sports-car proportions, dual electric motors, quattro all-wheel drive, advanced chassis technology, and high-performance battery system create a driving experience focused heavily on acceleration and handling.

Sport Luxury

Audi S e-tron GT represents the performance-oriented version of the e-tron GT. It adds greater power, enhanced acceleration, sportier chassis technology, and distinctive S design while retaining the luxurious grand-tourer character of the standard model.

Peak Electric Performance

Audi RS e-tron GT is the highest-performance interpretation of Audi’s electric grand tourer. Developed by Audi Sport, it combines extremely powerful electric motors, quattro all-wheel drive, advanced suspension, high-performance braking, and aggressive RS styling. It sits at the top of Audi’s electric performance hierarchy.
