Audi RS Class

RS Class

The RS Class is the expression of Audi’s performance DNA at full intensity. It combines race-bred hardware with premium luxury and tremendous pace.

Audi RS family

Compact Performance Flagship

Audi RS3 is the compact performance flagship of the A3 family. Its high-performance five-cylinder engine, quattro all-wheel drive, sport-tuned chassis, and aggressive styling give it an exceptionally dynamic character. The RS3 is available in Sportback and Sedan forms depending on the market.

Avant Performance Legend

Audi RS4 was the high-performance version of the A4 family. Known primarily for its Avant wagon configuration, the RS4 combined a powerful twin-turbocharged engine, quattro all-wheel drive, performance-oriented suspension, and practical cargo space. The RS4 name belongs to Audi's previous model generation following the transition from A4 to the new A5 range.

Modern RS Executive

Audi RS5 is the high-performance model of the new A5 family. It combines an aggressive design with powerful electrified performance, quattro all-wheel drive, advanced chassis technology, and a driver-focused interior. The RS5 is offered in Saloon and Avant configurations, providing a choice between performance-focused luxury and high-performance practicality.

Luxury Wagon Performance

Audi RS6 is one of the most recognizable models in the RS lineup. Traditionally offered as an Avant, the RS6 combines a large luxury wagon body with an extremely powerful drivetrain, quattro all-wheel drive, advanced suspension, and aggressive RS styling. It provides supercar-level performance while retaining substantial everyday practicality.

Fastback Grand Tourer

Audi RS7 is the high-performance counterpart to the A7 Sportback. Its sleek fastback design is combined with a powerful drivetrain, quattro all-wheel drive, performance suspension, large brakes, and an aggressive RS exterior. The RS7 is designed as a high-performance grand tourer with a strong emphasis on both speed and luxury.

Compact SUV Power

Audi RS Q3 brings RS performance into the compact SUV segment. It combines the practicality and elevated driving position of the Q3 with a high-performance engine, quattro all-wheel drive, sport suspension, and distinctive RS styling.

Flagship RS SUV

Audi RS Q8 is the performance flagship of the Q SUV family. It combines the size and luxury of the Q8 with extreme performance, quattro all-wheel drive, advanced chassis systems, large performance brakes, and an aggressive RS design. It delivers the character of a high-performance sports car while maintaining the presence and practicality of a large SUV.
